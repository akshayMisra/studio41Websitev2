The logline

Who we are

Studio41 is Singapore American School's student film production studio: a crew out of the Media Lab who write, shoot, and edit everything on this site.

We make FirstTake for High School Advisory, original series like Unmasked, Profiles and Hot Shots, hype videos for SAS teams, and live streams of the events you can't make it to. Students, teachers, and clubs are all welcome to bring us a story.
